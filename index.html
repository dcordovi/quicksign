Frequently asked questions

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser — you can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it keeps working. Your files never touch a server.

Is a drawn signature legally valid?

In many jurisdictions — including under the US ESIGN Act and the EU eIDAS regulation — simple electronic signatures are legally recognized for most everyday documents like leases, freelance contracts and internal forms. For high-stakes or notarized documents, check what signature level the receiving party requires.

How do I sign a PDF for free?

Drop your PDF into the box above, click "Add signature", draw it with your mouse or finger, drag it onto the signature line and press Download. No account needed.

Will there be a watermark on my signed PDF?

No watermarks and no quality loss — the signed file is your original PDF with just your signature embedded.

Can I sign a password-protected PDF?

Encrypted PDFs can't be processed — remove the password first using the tool that created the document, then drop the unlocked file here.

Can I save my signature for next time?

Yes — Premium lets you save drawn, typed or photographed signatures on your device so future documents take seconds. The signature itself is stored only in your own browser.
